The long version

How I got
here.

I started as the person who logged footage and made tea on ad sets in Mumbai. It was the best possible education.

Watching senior editors taught me the thing film school does not: that a cut is a decision about attention. Every frame you hold is a frame you are asking someone to give you. In 2017 I went independent, mostly because a founder in Berlin needed a brand film and did not care where the editor sat.

That first remote project turned into nine years of them. Somewhere along the way the work split into two halves — long-form storytelling where structure does the work, and short-form where the first second does. I care about both, and I think being good at one makes you better at the other.

These days it is mostly brand films, founder documentaries and short-form retainers, with colour and sound handled in house. Small by choice: you get me on your project, not a junior with my name on the invoice.

What I will not do

Unlimited revisions with no defined scope. Work without a contract. Cut something I think is dishonest. Take on a deadline I cannot hit — I would rather refer you to someone good than deliver something mediocre.
